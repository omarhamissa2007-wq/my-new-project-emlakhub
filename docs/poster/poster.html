01 · WHYProperty information is hard to reach.

The problem المشكلة

Scattered, slow property search

Property searching is fragmented and property management is manual. Buying, selling and renting need a clear presentation of information, trust, and organized documentation.

Our solution الحل

One centralized online platform

Users browse available houses and apartments and see the details quickly, in one place online. EmlakHub simplifies the process and reduces the time spent searching and managing property records.

02 · SEE ITFrom gallery to signed contract.من المعرض إلى العقد

Signing for a furnished flat for rent: details filled in from the account, the blank tenant contract to download, and the signed PDF uploaded.

  • Search and filterSale or rent, apartment or villa, rooms, and a max price.
  • Photos and detailsUp to 5 photos per home. Rent is shown per month.
  • Sign in three stepsDownload the contract, sign it, upload it. Buying or renting picks the right one.
